Transparent by design

How best-fit ranking works

  1. Evidence first. Dishes with restaurant-published nutrition rank above menu-only candidates.
  2. Your limits next. Known sodium, saturated fat, and meal calories are compared with the filters you choose.
  3. Substantial, not merely large. Meals meeting your calorie minimum get a modest boost, while sodium and saturated-fat ceilings still apply.
  4. Practical signals. Vegetable-forward dishes and useful modifications receive a modest boost.

Component-based totals (for customizable meals) are marked separately because real scoops and substitutions vary. Unknown values never become zero and never receive a “verified fit” label.

Data notes

What is—and is not—verified

Published Values copied from an official restaurant nutrition guide for the named serving.

Official components Total calculated from official ingredient values. Displayed with “≈” because serving scoops vary.

Estimate User-entered estimate. Never treated as official.

Unknown* Menu item is supported by an official menu page, but full nutrition was not found.

Discovery only The location is supported by either matched Montgomery County restaurant/inspection records or a current official area dining directory. Its menu has not yet been reviewed, so it has no DASH ranking.

Shared chain menu Official U.S. chain nutrition is linked to matching local locations. Availability must still be confirmed with that location.

The discovery catalog uses county records through October 23, 2026. A recent inspection record supports the location’s existence, but does not guarantee that it is currently open. Menus change; confirm availability before ordering.
